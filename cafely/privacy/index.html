Privacy Policy

Effective November 18, 2026

Overview

Cafely is a focus timer set in illustrated cafés in real cities. It does not require an account and contains no advertising, analytics, crash-reporting or tracking SDK.

What is stored on your device

Your study sessions and totals, the café and seat you last chose, your sound and music settings, language, and the postcards and certificates you have earned are stored on your iPhone only. They are not sent to a developer-operated server. Deleting the app removes this data.

People in the cafés

The other guests you see in each café are illustrations that are part of the scene. Cafely does not show your presence to anyone else, and no one else can see you.

Lock Screen and Dynamic Island

While a session is running, Cafely can show your timer on the Lock Screen and in the Dynamic Island as a Live Activity. iOS shows and removes it on your device; it is not sent anywhere. You can turn it off in the app or in Settings › Cafely.

Sharing

When you choose to share a postcard or certificate, Cafely creates the image on your iPhone and hands it to the iOS share sheet. What happens next is up to you and the app you share to. The name on a certificate is only what you type, and it stays on your device.

What is not collected

Cafely does not ask for your email address, phone number, location or contacts. It does not use your camera or microphone, does not read your photos or files, does not build a profile of you and does not track you across other apps or websites.

Network use

Cafely works without a network connection. All scenes, sounds and music are inside the app. It contacts the network only through Apple’s StoreKit service to show prices, start or restore a subscription or purchase, and check your subscription status.

Subscriptions and purchases

Subscriptions and the one-time purchase are processed by Apple. Cafely checks your entitlement on your device and never receives or stores your payment details. Apple processes App Store transactions under Apple’s own privacy terms.

Children

Cafely collects no personal data from anyone, including children.

Your choices

  • Turn the Lock Screen timer on or off in the app.
  • Delete Cafely to remove all data it stored on your device.
  • Manage or cancel your subscription in Settings › your name › Subscriptions.

Changes

This policy may be updated when Cafely’s features or data practices change, for example if a future version adds studying with friends. The effective date above identifies the current version.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to cloudrise.support@gmail.com.
