Cafely help

How do I start studying?

Swipe left or right on the street to travel between cities, tap Enter café, pick an empty seat and tap Sit here. Choose Count up or Countdown and tap Start.

Can I pause?

Yes. Tap pause any time, and tap again to carry on. Tap stop to end the session; the time is added to today’s total.

Who are the other people in the café?

They are illustrated guests that belong to each café, there to keep you company. Version 1.0 does not connect you with other real people.

How do I change the sounds?

Tap the speaker button while you study. You can mute everything, or switch café ambience, music, weather and study sounds on and off and set each volume. Tap the music note to choose a different track for that city.

The timer isn’t on my Lock Screen

Check that the Lock Screen timer is on in the app’s settings, and that Live Activities are allowed in Settings › Cafely. iOS ends Live Activities after about 8 hours.

How do postcards and certificates work?

Finish your first focus session of 25 minutes or more in a café to receive that city’s postcard. Certificates arrive at 10, 50, 100 and 500 hours of total study. Open them from the menu under Achievements to view or share.

What does it cost?

Cafely Plus unlocks every café, sound and feature. Choose Monthly (US$3.99), Yearly (US$24.99, starting with a 7-day free trial) or the Founder lifetime purchase (US$39.99, one-time, available for a limited time). Prices in other countries are set by the App Store in local currency.

How do I cancel?

Open Settings › your name › Subscriptions › Cafely and tap Cancel Subscription at least 24 hours before the renewal date. You keep access until the end of the period you paid for. If you cancel during the free trial, you are not charged.

How do I restore a purchase?

On the Cafely Plus screen tap Restore Purchases. Use the same Apple Account that made the original purchase.

Refunds

Purchases are handled by Apple. Request a refund at reportaproblem.apple.com using the Apple Account that made the purchase.

Music credits

The full list of music and sound credits is in the app under Menu › Sounds › Credits.

Contact support

Email cloudrise.support@gmail.com. Include your iPhone model, your iOS version, the app version and a short description of the problem.
