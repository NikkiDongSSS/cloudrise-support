Privacy Policy

Effective November 17, 2026

Overview

Odd Catch is a focus timer: your raft drifts out while you focus, and you reel in a fish when the time is up. It is made by Cloud Rise. It does not require an account and contains no advertising, analytics, crash-reporting or tracking SDK, and no other third-party SDK.

What is stored on your device

Your progress (fish caught, your aquarium, coins, items, collection, islands and focus history), your sound settings, your language choice and whether you have Odd Catch Plus are stored on your iPhone only. They are not sent to a developer-operated server. Deleting the app removes this data.

Notifications

The first time you cast off, Odd Catch asks whether it may send notifications. If you allow it, the app schedules a local notification on your iPhone to tell you when your raft is back. It is not sent from a server. You can turn notifications off in Settings › Odd Catch › Notifications.

Lock Screen, Dynamic Island and widgets

During a voyage, Odd Catch shows the remaining time on the Lock Screen and in the Dynamic Island as a Live Activity. The Home Screen widget shows today’s focus minutes and the fish in your aquarium, read from a small summary the app keeps in storage shared with its widget on your iPhone. iOS displays all of this on your device; nothing is sent anywhere.

Photos and sharing

When you tap save on a postcard or medal card, Odd Catch asks for permission to add the image to your Photos. It can only add images; it cannot see or read your photo library. When you choose to share a card, the image is created on your iPhone and handed to the iOS share sheet. What happens next is up to you and the app you share to.

What is not collected

Odd Catch does not ask for your name, email address, phone number, location or contacts. It does not use your camera or microphone, does not read your photos or files, does not build a profile of you and does not track you across other apps or websites.

Network use

Odd Catch works without a network connection. All fish, scenes, music and sounds are inside the app. It contacts the network only through Apple’s StoreKit service to show prices, make or restore a purchase, and check your subscription status.

Subscriptions and purchases

Odd Catch Plus subscriptions and the lifetime purchase are processed by Apple. Odd Catch checks your purchase on your device and never receives or stores your payment details. Apple processes App Store transactions under Apple’s own privacy terms.

Children

Odd Catch collects no personal data from anyone, including children.

Your choices

  • Allow or turn off notifications and Live Activities in Settings › Odd Catch.
  • Delete Odd Catch to remove all data it stored on your device.
  • Manage or cancel your subscription in Settings › your name › Subscriptions.

Changes

This policy may be updated when Odd Catch’s features or data practices change. The effective date above identifies the current version.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to cloudrise.support@gmail.com.
