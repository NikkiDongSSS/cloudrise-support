Odd Catch help

How does a voyage work?

Tap Cast Off, choose how long you want to focus and your raft drifts out. When the time is up the raft comes back with a fish on the line. Keep it in your aquarium or sell it for coins to spend in the shop.

What does Odd Catch Plus include?

Plus adds more music, exclusive tents, a starlight rod, foil card frames, raft decorations and the Home Screen widget. Fishing, coins and the collection are fully playable for free.

Subscription or lifetime?

Choose Monthly, Yearly (new subscribers can start with a 7-day free trial) or Lifetime. Monthly and Yearly renew automatically until you cancel. Lifetime is a one-time purchase that unlocks Plus for good, with nothing to renew. Prices are shown in the app in your local currency.

How do I cancel or manage my subscription?

Open Settings › your name › Subscriptions › Odd Catch and tap Cancel Subscription at least 24 hours before the renewal date. You keep Plus until the end of the period you paid for. If you cancel during the free trial, you are not charged. Subscribers can also tap Manage Subscription on the Plus screen.

How do I restore a purchase?

Tap Restore Purchases in the app’s Settings or on the Plus screen. Use the same Apple Account that made the original purchase.

Refunds

Purchases are handled by Apple. Request a refund at reportaproblem.apple.com using the Apple Account that made the purchase.

How do I change the language?

Open Settings in the app and choose System, English or 简体中文 next to Language. The change applies right away.

Can the sounds keep playing when my iPhone is locked?

Yes. Music and ocean sounds keep playing on the Lock Screen and while you use other apps, as long as Keep playing when locked is on in the app’s Settings. Turn it off if you only want sound while the app is open. Another app that starts playing audio can pause Odd Catch’s sound.

The timer isn’t on my Lock Screen

Check that Live Activities are allowed in Settings › Odd Catch. The Live Activity appears while a voyage is running and goes away when the raft is back.

I didn’t get a notification when my raft came back

Check that notifications are allowed in Settings › Odd Catch › Notifications, and that Focus or Do Not Disturb isn’t silencing them. If the app is open when the raft arrives, you hear the bell in the app instead.

Where is my progress saved? Will I lose it if I delete the app?

Your progress is saved only on your iPhone. There is no account, so if you delete the app your fish, coins and collection are removed with it. They come back only if you restore your iPhone from an iCloud or computer backup made while the app was installed. Odd Catch Plus is tied to your Apple Account, so tap Restore Purchases after reinstalling.

Music credits

Music and sound credits are in the app under Settings › About & Privacy.

Contact support

Email cloudrise.support@gmail.com. Include your iPhone model, your iOS version, the app version and a short description of the problem.
