Privacy Policy

Effective November 2, 2026

Overview

Pinkitty puts your own notes and checklists on the iPhone Lock Screen. It does not require an account and contains no advertising, analytics, crash-reporting or tracking SDK.

What is stored on your device

Your notes, checklists, templates, chosen paper and lettering, and app settings are stored on your iPhone only, in the app’s own storage shared with its Lock Screen widget. They are not sent to a developer-operated server. Deleting the app removes this data.

What shows on your Lock Screen

Notes you pin appear on your Lock Screen as Live Activities and in the Pinkitty widget. Anyone who can see your Lock Screen can read them, so do not pin anything private, or tap the eye on the card to turn the writing into little fish until you tap it again. iOS shows and removes these cards; Pinkitty does not send them anywhere else.

What is not collected

Pinkitty does not ask for your name, email address, phone number, location or contacts. It does not read your photos, files or calendar. It does not build a profile of you and does not track you across other apps or websites.

Network use

Pinkitty works without a network connection. It contacts the network only through Apple’s own StoreKit service when you open the purchase screen, buy, or restore a purchase.

Notifications

If you schedule a note for a set time, iOS asks once whether Pinkitty may send notifications. The notification is created on your iPhone; nothing is sent to a server.

Backups

Exporting a backup creates a file that you choose where to save, for example in Files or iCloud Drive. Importing reads a file you pick. Pinkitty does not upload backups anywhere.

Purchases

The optional one-time purchase is processed by Apple through StoreKit. Pinkitty checks the purchase on your device and never receives or stores your payment-card details. Apple processes App Store transactions under Apple’s own privacy terms.

Children

Pinkitty collects no personal data from anyone, including children.

Your choices

  • Swipe a card away on the Lock Screen, or take a note off in the app, at any time.
  • Delete notes and templates in the app.
  • Delete Pinkitty to remove all data it stored on your device.
  • Manage or request a refund for the purchase through your Apple Account.

Changes

This policy may be updated when Pinkitty’ features or data practices change. The effective date above identifies the current version.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to cloudrise.support@gmail.com.
