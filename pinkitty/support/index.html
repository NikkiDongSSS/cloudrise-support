Pinkitty help

How do I put a note on my Lock Screen?

Write on the page and tap Pin to Lock Screen (or the pin key above the keyboard). The page tears off and shows on your Lock Screen straight away. Nothing needs to be set up.

My note isn’t on the Lock Screen

Open Settings › Pinkitty and make sure Live Activities are on. Also check Settings › Face ID & Passcode › Allow Access When Locked › Live Activities.

Why did my card disappear after a while?

iOS takes Lock Screen cards down after about 8 hours. Open Pinkitty and your pinned notes go back up.

Can I tick items off on the Lock Screen?

Yes. Tap a box on the card to cross the item off. Swipe a card left and tap Clear to take it off.

What is free, and what does the purchase unlock?

Free: write as many notes as you like, pin as often as you like, with one card on the Lock Screen at a time, and keep up to 3 templates. A one-time purchase unlocks up to 5 cards on the Lock Screen together, unlimited templates, hiding notes on the Lock Screen (as little fish, yarn balls or toy mice), posting a note at a set time, and backups. There is no subscription and no advertising.

How do templates work?

On the Write page, open ••• › Save as Template. A blank page then offers your templates along its bottom; tap one to fill the page with its lines, unticked.

How do I restore a purchase?

Open the Me tab and choose Restore Purchase. Use the same Apple Account that made the original purchase.

Refunds

Purchases are handled by Apple. Request a refund at reportaproblem.apple.com using the Apple Account that made the purchase.

Contact support

Email cloudrise.support@gmail.com. Include your iPhone model, your iOS version, the app version and a short description of the problem.
