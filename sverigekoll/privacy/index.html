Privacy Policy

Effective September 16, 2026

Overview

Sverigekoll is a study app for the Swedish citizenship test. It does not require an account and contains no advertising, analytics, crash-reporting or tracking SDK.

What is stored on your device

Your answers, chapter progress, exam history, mistake list and app settings are stored on your device only. They are not sent to a developer-operated server. Deleting the app removes this data.

What is not collected

Sverigekoll does not ask for your name, email address, phone number, location or contacts. It does not read your photos, files or calendar. It does not build a profile of you and does not track you across other apps or websites.

Network use

The study material and questions are bundled inside the app, so learning works offline. The app contacts the network only through Apple's own StoreKit service when you open the purchase screen, buy, or restore a purchase.

Purchases

The optional one-time purchase that unlocks all chapters is processed by Apple through StoreKit. Sverigekoll checks the purchase entitlement on your device and never receives or stores your payment-card details. Apple processes App Store transactions under Apple's own privacy terms.

Reporting a problem

If you use the report button in the app, iOS opens your own mail app with a message addressed to the developer. Nothing is sent until you press send, and you decide what the message contains. Mail you send this way is used only to answer you and to correct the content you reported.

Children

Sverigekoll is a study tool for an adult citizenship test. It is not directed at children and collects no personal data from anyone.

Your choices

  • Reset your progress from the app's own settings where available.
  • Delete Sverigekoll to remove all data it stored on your device.
  • Manage or request a refund for the purchase through your Apple Account.

Changes

This policy may be updated when Sverigekoll's features or data practices change. The effective date above identifies the current version.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to cloudrise.support@gmail.com.
